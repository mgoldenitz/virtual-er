### 3. Feedback on Fixed Sentences

- **F6** ("Please return now. Staff will review your visit if you do not respond."): "review your visit" is slightly vague; a patient might not realize it could mean losing their place or being marked a no-show. It fits the non-clinical tone, but lacks a clear consequence.

### 4. Missing States

- **Offline / lost connection:** no message for when the phone loses internet (for example in a subway). Suggested banner: "You are offline. Reconnect to the internet to see your status." Otherwise the patient may look at a stale place in line without realizing they missed the call-back.
