Visit lifecycle

Every status a visit can have, from "checked in from home" to closed, and what the patient's phone, the staff screen and the dashboard show at each one. Times on this page are the demo settings in the app; a hospital's clinical lead would set the real ones.

Nurse triage (the check-in)

a step, not a status

Patient phone

  • Home patients show their QR code; desk patients get a printed slip with a QR code, the 6-character code and the full link under "Or type this link into your phone's browser:"
  • Only the long token in the QR code or link opens a visit. Typing the short code on /patient does not.

Staff screen

  • Check-in form: CTAS 4 or 5 (the nurse decides, never the app), the eligibility checklist (has a phone, can return within 20 minutes, agrees to wait off-site) and the language.
  • Three buttons: Start off-site waiting (needs all three ticks), Offered, declined, and Not eligible.
  • Start off-site waiting shows the slip.

Dashboard

  • Offered off-site waiting, shown as "12 of 14 triaged (86%)"
  • Accepted, shown as "11 of 12 offered (92%)"

Patient phone

  • Heading "Status: Waiting", then "Your place in line:" with the number.
  • "Your place can change if more urgent patients arrive. The most urgent patients are always seen first."
  • "Estimated time until you're called back:" a range such as "40 to 60 minutes", then "This estimate is simulated for the demo."
  • 30 minutes after triage or the last tap: "Are you still following your visit?", the "I'm still here" button and "This tells staff you are still waiting. It is not a check of your health." After a tap: "Thank you."

Staff screen

  • Off-site list, ordered by triage time: code, CTAS level, minutes waited.
  • A missed "I'm still here" (10 minutes after the prompt) flags the row.
  • Recall button per patient, plus "Recall all off-site patients" with a confirmation step.
  • Remove from list (Left or No-show) sits inside a section that must be opened first.

Closed outcomes

closed

Each of these sets closed_at in the same save. The 911 line stays on the patient phone in every case.

Arrived arrived

  • Phone: "Status: Ready at the desk" "Staff know you are back. Please stay in the waiting area until you are called."
  • Survey, for patients who waited off-site and arrived: "How would you rate your waiting experience?" from "0 = Very poor" to "10 = Excellent", with "Answering is optional. Your name is not stored."
  • Staff: moves to Recently closed, with Undo.
  • Dashboard: Average minutes from recall to arrival; Survey responses, Average score, Scored 9 or 10.

Removed removed

  • Reason Left or No-show.
  • Phone: "Your off-site wait has ended" "Staff have ended off-site waiting for this visit. If you still need care, please come to the emergency department desk."
  • Staff: Recently closed, with Undo. A stale Undo button cannot reverse a newer action.
  • Dashboard: Left; No-shows.

Unable to reach unable_to_reach

  • Only after the 30-minute flag and a nurse's confirmation. Not recorded as left without being seen.
  • Phone: "Your off-site wait has ended" "Staff could not reach you, so this visit has left the off-site line. If you still need care, please come to the emergency department desk."
  • If the patient arrives later, staff reopen it: Reassessment, then triage again.
  • Dashboard: Unable to reach (nurse confirmed).

Declined declined

  • Offered off-site waiting and said no; waits inside as usual.
  • Desk patients get no slip. A patient who started from home sees "Status: Waiting in the emergency department" "You are waiting inside the hospital. Staff will call you when they are ready."
  • Staff: "Waiting on-site: offered, declined" list for 6 hours (see below).
  • Dashboard: Declined.

Not eligible not_eligible

  • Not offered; waits inside as usual. Final: it cannot switch.
  • Phone (home starts only): the same on-site message as Declined.
  • Dashboard: taken out of the base for Offered off-site waiting (SPEC definition).

Back for reassessment needs_reassessment

  • Not closed: a reopened unable-to-reach visit, waiting for triage again.
  • Phone: "Please go to the triage desk" "A nurse needs to check you again before you go back in line."
  • Dashboard: Back for reassessment (Now).

Declined, then switched to off-site

declined → waitingadded 2026-11-19, PR #4

Rules

  • Only within 6 hours of declining, and only while the site is not paused. The database refuses it otherwise.
  • The patient keeps the place in line from the original triage time, because they have been waiting inside since then.
  • The "I'm still here" clock starts at the switch, so the first prompt comes 30 minutes later.

Staff screen

  • "Waiting on-site: offered, declined" list: code, CTAS level, how long ago they declined, and the place they would get.
  • Switch to off-site waiting opens the same 3-item checklist and the language; Start off-site waiting saves it and shows the slip.
  • Undo switch puts it back to declined, until the patient is recalled.

Patient phone and dashboard

  • Phone: scans the new slip and sees the "Status: Waiting" screen. Screen readers hear "You are now in the off-site line. This page shows your place in line."
  • Dashboard: the visit counts as offered and accepted, no longer as declined.

True at every step

  • Every patient screen shows "If your symptoms get worse, call 911 or come back to the emergency department now."
  • Every page shows "Concept prototype. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Trillium Health Partners. Demo data only."
  • No names, health card numbers, phone numbers or symptoms are stored. A visit has a 6-character code and a long secret token.
  • Visits are deleted after 24 hours; their numbers move into the daily totals the dashboard reads.
  • The app never makes a clinical decision. Staff choose the CTAS level and eligibility.
